## Constraints

- University facilities and supervision requirements.
- Available budget and component lead times.
- Manufacturing capability.
- Battery and propulsion limitations.
- Software and electronics development time.
- Applicable aviation and university rules.

Each requirement should have a measurable target and verification method.
