# Experiments

Every significant experiment should record enough information for another team member to reproduce or evaluate it.

## Template

### Objective
What are we trying to determine?

### Hypothesis
What do we expect?

### Setup
Equipment, configuration and test conditions.

### Variables
- Independent:
- Dependent:
- Controlled:

### Procedure
Numbered test steps.

### Measurements
Record raw data and units.

### Result
Include calculations, plots or other evidence.

### Conclusion
What did the experiment show?

### Limitations
What could affect confidence in the result?

### Next action
State the resulting engineering decision or follow-up task.

**Safety:** powered propulsion and flight experiments require the agreed university risk assessment, supervision and test procedures.
